# Margin values for eligible noncash margin collateral

Source: 12 CFR Appendix B to Part 45, Table B, "Margin Values for Eligible Noncash Margin Collateral".
URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/12/appendix-B_to_part_45
Retrieved: 2026-11-20. The page shows no effective date.
Numbers are copied from the page. Asset-class labels are shortened. The full wording is at the URL.
This is the US rule. It has no rating buckets, no cash row, and no currency-mismatch row.

## Table B

Eligible government and related debt, residual maturity under one year: discount 0.5 percent.
Eligible government and related debt, residual maturity one to five years: discount 2.0 percent.
Eligible government and related debt, residual maturity over five years: discount 4.0 percent.
Eligible GSE debt not identified in section 45.6(a)(2)(iv) or (b)(5), residual maturity under one year: discount 1.0 percent.
Eligible GSE debt not identified in section 45.6(a)(2)(iv) or (b)(5), residual maturity one to five years: discount 4.0 percent.
Eligible GSE debt not identified in section 45.6(a)(2)(iv) or (b)(5), residual maturity over five years: discount 8.0 percent.
Other eligible publicly traded debt, residual maturity under one year: discount 1.0 percent.
Other eligible publicly traded debt, residual maturity one to five years: discount 4.0 percent.
Other eligible publicly traded debt, residual maturity over five years: discount 8.0 percent.
Equities included in the S&P 500 or a related index: discount 15.0 percent.
Equities included in the S&P 1500 Composite or a related index but not the S&P 500 or a related index: discount 25.0 percent.
Gold: discount 15.0 percent.
