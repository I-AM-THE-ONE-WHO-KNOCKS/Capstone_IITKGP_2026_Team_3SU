 AMENDED AND RESTATED 

 PLEDGE AND SECURITY AGREEMENT 

 This AMENDED AND RESTATED PLEDGE AND SECURITY AGREEMENT (this “ A&R Security Agreement ”), dated as of December 16, 2025, is made by Hawaii Renewables, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “ Company ”), in favor of Wells Fargo Bank, N.A., a national banking association (the “ Secured Party ”).

 RECITALS :

 WHEREAS , the Company and the Secured Party entered into that certain Pledge and Security Agreement, dated as of October 2, 2025 (as amended prior to the date hereof, the “ Existing Security Agreement ”), in connection with (i) that certain ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, dated as of October 2, 2025 (as amended, amended and restated, supplemented and otherwise modified from time to time, the “ ISDA Master Agreement ”), between the Company and the Secured Party, including the Credit Support Annex to the Schedule thereto, and (ii) the Framework Agreement, dated as of October 2, 2025, between the Company and the Secured Party (as amended, amended and restated, supplemented and otherwise modified from time to time, the “ Framework Agreement ”);

 WHEREAS , the Company and the Secured Party entered into that certain Letter of Credit Facility Agreement, dated as of the date hereof, between the Company and the Secured Party (as amended, amended and restated, supplemented and otherwise modified from time to time, the “ LC Facility Agreement ”);

 WHEREAS , pursuant to the Transaction Documents (as defined in the Framework Agreement), (i) the Company and the Secured Party may enter into swap transactions from time to time in respect of certain notional quantities of soybean oil and crude oil and (ii) the Company may request that the Secured Party issue letters of credit in connection with certain shipments of soybean oil and crude oil pursuant to the LC Facility Agreement, subject to the conditions set forth therein, in the case of clauses (i) and (ii), conditioned upon, among other things, the execution and delivery of this A&R Security Agreement; and

 WHEREAS , the Company has determined that the execution, delivery and performance of this A&R Security Agreement directly benefit, and are in the best interest of, the Company.

 NOW, THEREFORE , in order to comply with the terms and conditions of the Transaction Documents, for and in consideration of the agreements herein contained and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of all of which being hereby acknowledged, the Company and the Secured Party hereby agree as follows:
