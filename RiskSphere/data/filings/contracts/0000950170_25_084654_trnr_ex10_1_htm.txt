 SECURITIES PURCHASE AGREEMENT 
 This SECURITIES PURCHASE AGREEMENT (the “ Agreement ”), dated as of June 10, 2025, is by and among Interactive Strength Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices located at 1005 Congress Avenue, Suite 925, Austin, TX 78701 (the “ Company ”), Interactive Strength Treasury LLC, its wholly owned subsidiary, a Delaware limited liability company (the “ Treasury Subsidiary ”, and together with the Company, the “ Borrowers ”), and each of the investors listed on the Schedule of Buyers attached hereto (individually, a “ Buyer ” and collectively, the “ Buyers ”). 
 RECITALS 
 A. The Borrowers and each Buyer is executing and delivering this Agreement in reliance upon the exemption from securities registration afforded by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “ 1933 Act ”), and Rule 506(b) of Regulation D (“ Regulation D ”) as promulgated by the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (the “ SEC ”) under the 1933 Act. 

 B. The Borrowers have authorized one or more new series of secured convertible exchangeable notes of the Borrower, up to the aggregate original principal amount of $500,000,000, substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A (the “ Notes ”), which Notes shall be convertible into shares of Common Stock (the shares of Common Stock issuable pursuant to the terms of the Notes, including, without limitation, upon conversion or otherwise, collectively, the “ Conversion Shares ”) and exchangeable into certain FET (as defined in the Notes), in each case, in accordance with the terms of the Notes. 

 C. Each Buyer wishes to purchase, and the Borrower wish to sell at the Initial Closing (as defined below), upon the terms and conditions stated in this Agreement, a Note in the aggregate original principal amount as set forth opposite such Buyer’s name in column (3) on the Schedule of Buyers (which aggregate principal amount for all Buyers shall not exceed $55,555,556) (each an “ Initial Note ”, and collectively, the “ Initial Notes ”)(the Conversion Shares issuable pursuant to the terms of the Initial Notes, collectively, the “ Initial Conversion Shares ”). 

 D. Subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement, each Buyer, severally, may require the Borrowers to participate in one or more Additional Closings (as defined below) for the purchase by such Buyer, and the sale by the Borrowers, of one or more Notes with an aggregate original principal amount for all Additional Closings not to exceed the maximum aggregate principal amount as set forth opposite such Buyer’s name in column (4) on the Schedule of Buyers (which aggregate principal amount for all Buyers for all Additional Closings shall not exceed $444,444,445 (or such other amount as the Borrowers and each Buyer shall mutually agree in writing)) (each an “ Additional Note ”, and collectively, the “ Additional Notes ”, and together with the Initial Notes, the “ Notes ”)(the Conversion Shares issuable pursuant to the terms of the Additional Notes, collectively, the “ Additional Conversion Shares ”, and collectively with the Initial Conversion Shares, the “ Conversion Shares ”). 

 E. The Notes and the Conversion Shares are collectively referred to herein as the “ Securities .” 

 F. At the Closing, the parties hereto shall execute and deliver a Registration Rights Agreement, in the form attached hereto as Exhibit B (the “ Registration Rights Agreement ”), pursuant to which the Company has agreed to provide certain registration rights with respect to the Registrable Securities (as defined in the Registration Rights Agreement), under the 1933 Act and the rules and regulations promulgated thereunder, and applicable state securities laws. 

 G. The Notes will be secured by (a) a first priority perfected security interest in all of the existing and future assets of the Treasury Subsidiary (defined below), including the FET Collateral (as defined in the Security Agreement) as evidenced by a security agreement, in the form attached hereto as Exhibit C (as amended, restated, supplemented and modified from time to time, the “ Security Agreement ”), (b) a custodian control agreement, in form and substance satisfactory to the Collateral Agent, in respect of each Blocked ATW Custodial Account (as defined in the Security Agreement), each Blocked DWF Custodial Account (as defined in the Security Agreement), and each General Custodial Account (collectively, the “ Primary Custodian Control Agreements ”), (c) that certain Backstop Agreement, by and among FET US I LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“ Fetch ”) and the Borrowers providing for, among other things, the grant by Fetch to the Treasury Subsidiary of a valid, enforceable, and perfected security interest in the Backstop FET Collateral (as defined in the Security Agreement) to secure the Total Backstop Amount (as defined in the Security Agreement), in the form attached hereto as Exhibit D (the “ Backstop Agreement ”) and (d) a custodian control agreement, in form and substance satisfactory to the Collateral Agent, in respect of each Blocked Fetch ATW Custodial Account (as defined in the Security Agreement) and each Blocked Fetch DWF Custodial Account (as defined in the Security Agreement), each of which, together with the Backstop Agreement, shall be collateral assigned to the Collateral Agent, for the ratable benefit of itself and the Noteholders (as defined in the Security Agreement) (collectively, the “ Fetch Custodian Control Agreements ”, and together with the Primary Custodian Control Agreements, the “ Custodian Control Agreements ”; the Custodian Control Agreements, together with the Security Agreement, the Primary Custodian Control Agreements, the Backstop Agreement, and the other security documents and agreements entered into in connection with this Agreement and each of such other documents and agreements, including, without limitation, any agreement collateral assigning the Backstop Agreement and the Custodian Control Agreements in respect of the Blocked Fetch DWF Custodial Account and the Blocked Fetch DWF Custodial Account to the Collateral Agent, as each of the foregoing may be amended or modified from time to time pursuant to the terms thereof, collectively, the “ Security Documents ”). 

 H. Concurrently herewith, the Borrowers and each Buyer, separately, are entering into that certain Master Netting Agreement, in substantially the form attached hereto as Exhibit E (each, a “ Master Netting Agreement ”), to provide further clarification of their rights (but not, in the case of each such Buyer only, its obligation) to Net (as defined below) certain Obligations (as defined in the Master Netting Agreement) arising under and across this Agreement, the Notes and the Tokens Agreement (as defined in the Master Netting Agreement) (collectively, the “ Underlying Agreements ”) and to treat the Master Netting Agreement, this Agreement and the other Underlying Agreements as a single agreement for the purposes set forth herein and to treat this Agreement each as a “securities contract” (11 U.S.C. § 741) or other similar agreements.
