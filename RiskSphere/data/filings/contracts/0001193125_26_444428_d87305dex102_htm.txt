 This Crude Oil Supply Agreement (this “ Agreement ”) is entered into on November 2nd, 2026 (the “ Effective Date ”), between
Trafigura Trading LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“ Trafigura ”), and MND Holdco, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“ CVR ”). Trafigura and CVR are each referred to individually herein as a
“ Party ” or collectively as the “ Parties ”. 
 WHEREAS , Affiliates of CVR are, concurrently with the execution
hereof, entering into that certain Equity and Asset Purchase Agreement, by and among Marathon Petroleum Company LP, Tesoro Refining & Marketing Company LLC, Western Refining Southwest LLC, Western Refining Terminals LLC, Marathon Petroleum
Supply and Trading LLC, and CVR Energy, Inc. (the “ Purchase Agreement ”) pursuant to which the Purchasers will acquire, directly or indirectly, 100% of the issued and outstanding membership interests in Saint Paul Park Refining Co.
LLC, 1,700 common units, representing 17% of the issued and outstanding common interests of Minnesota Pipe Line Company, LLC and 1,700 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, representing 17% of the issued and outstanding shares of MPL
Investments, Inc. and certain other assets relating to the petroleum refineries located in Mandan, North Dakota (the “ Mandan Refinery ”) and St. Paul Park, Minnesota (the “ SPP Refinery ”); 

 WHEREAS , concurrently with the execution hereof, Trafigura and CVR Supply & Trading, LLC, are entering into [***], and the Parties intend that
this Agreement and [***] operate together as a single crude oil intermediation arrangement; 
 WHEREAS , Trafigura is in the business of purchasing
and selling crude oil and refined products; and

inventories of, and thereafter supply, and CVR will purchase, Crude Oil for processing at the Refineries pursuant to the terms defined herein, upon the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement.
